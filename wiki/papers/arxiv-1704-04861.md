MobileNets are small, low-latency convolutional networks for mobile and
embedded vision, built from depthwise separable convolutions. Two global
hyperparameters, a width multiplier and a resolution multiplier, let a model
builder trade accuracy for latency and size [src:arxiv_1704.04861 p.1]. The
paper tests them on ImageNet and on detection, fine-grained classification,
face attributes and geo-localization.

Howard, Zhu, Chen, Kalenichenko, Wang, Weyand, Andreetto, Adam. *MobileNets:
Efficient Convolutional Neural Networks for Mobile Vision Applications.*
arXiv:1704.04861, 2017.

## Contribution

- **Latency first.** The architecture aims mainly at low latency and yields
  small networks as a side effect. Many earlier small-network papers looked
  only at size [src:arxiv_1704.04861 p.1].
- **Two shrinking knobs.** A width multiplier α and a resolution multiplier ρ
  define a family of models [src:arxiv_1704.04861 p.4].

## Method

- **[[depthwise-separable-convolution]].** A standard convolution is split
  into a depthwise convolution, one filter per input channel, and a 1×1
  pointwise convolution that combines channels [src:arxiv_1704.04861 p.2].
  With 3×3 kernels this needs 8–9× less computation than a standard
  convolution [src:arxiv_1704.04861 p.3].
- **Structure.** A full convolution first, then depthwise separable layers,
  each depthwise and pointwise layer followed by batch normalization and ReLU.
  Downsampling uses strided depthwise convolutions, and the network ends with
  average pooling, a fully connected layer and softmax
  [src:arxiv_1704.04861 p.3].
- **Built for fast dense math.** 95% of computation and 75% of parameters sit
  in 1×1 convolutions, which run directly as GEMM without im2col reordering
  [src:arxiv_1704.04861 p.3]. By layer type, 1×1 convolutions take 94.86% of
  mult-adds and 74.59% of parameters; the fully connected layer has 24.33% of
  parameters [src:arxiv_1704.04861 p.4].
- **Training.** TensorFlow with RMSprop and asynchronous gradient descent. It
  uses less regularization and augmentation than large models do, and little
  or no weight decay on depthwise filters [src:arxiv_1704.04861 p.4].
- **Width multiplier α.** Scales the channels of every layer and reduces cost
  and parameters by about α² [src:arxiv_1704.04861 p.4].
- **Resolution multiplier ρ.** Set through the input resolution (224, 192, 160
  or 128) and reduces cost by ρ² [src:arxiv_1704.04861 p.5].
- **One layer as an example.** A 14×14×512 layer costs 462M mult-adds as a
  standard convolution, 52.3M depthwise separable, 29.6M with α = 0.75, and
  15.1M with ρ = 0.714 [src:arxiv_1704.04861 p.5].

## Results

All ImageNet accuracies are top-1 as reported in the paper.

- **Depthwise vs full convolution.** 70.6% accuracy with 569M mult-adds and
  4.2M parameters, against 71.7% with 4866M and 29.3M
  [src:arxiv_1704.04861 p.5].
- **Thinner beats shallower.** At similar cost, a thinner model is 3% more
  accurate than a shallower one (68.4% vs 65.3%) [src:arxiv_1704.04861 p.5].
- **Width sweep.** Accuracy for α = 1, 0.75, 0.5, 0.25 is 70.6%, 68.4%, 63.7%
  and 50.6%, falling smoothly until α = 0.25 [src:arxiv_1704.04861 p.5].
- **Resolution sweep.** Accuracy at 224, 192, 160 and 128 pixels is 70.6%,
  69.1%, 67.2% and 64.4% [src:arxiv_1704.04861 p.5].
- **Against larger models.** MobileNet is nearly as accurate as VGG16 (70.6% vs
  71.5%) while 32× smaller and 27× cheaper. It is more accurate than GoogLeNet
  (69.8%) with more than 2.5× less computation [src:arxiv_1704.04861 p.6].
- **Small model.** 0.50 MobileNet-160 scores 60.2%, 4% better than AlexNet
  while 45× smaller [src:arxiv_1704.04861 p.6].
- **Other tasks.** Stanford Dogs: 83.3% against 84% for Inception V3
  [src:arxiv_1704.04861 p.6]. Face attributes: a distilled MobileNet matches
  the baseline's mean AP with 1% of the mult-adds [src:arxiv_1704.04861 p.7].
  COCO with SSD 300: 19.3% mAP with 1.2 billion mult-adds
  [src:arxiv_1704.04861 p.7].

## Limitations

- **Very small models.** Accuracy jumps down at α = 0.25
  [src:arxiv_1704.04861 p.5].
- **Detection.** MobileNet trails VGG in mAP, for example 19.8% vs 25.7% for
  Faster-RCNN at 600 pixels, though at a fraction of the cost
  [src:arxiv_1704.04861 p.7].
- **Hand-tuned.** The multipliers are set by hand. [[arxiv-1905-11946]] later
  proposes scaling width, depth and resolution together.
