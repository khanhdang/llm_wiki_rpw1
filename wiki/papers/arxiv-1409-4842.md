This paper introduces the Inception architecture and GoogLeNet, a 22-layer
network built from it. GoogLeNet set the state of the art for both
classification and detection at ILSVRC 2014. The design aims to use compute
well, making the network deeper and wider under a fixed computational budget
[src:arxiv_1409.4842 p.1].

Szegedy, Liu, Jia, Sermanet, Reed, Anguelov, Erhan, Vanhoucke, Rabinovich.
*Going deeper with convolutions.* arXiv:1409.4842, 2014.

## Contribution

- The [[inception-module]]: parallel 1×1, 3×3 and 5×5 convolutions plus a
  pooling branch, with 1×1 convolutions that reduce dimensions before the
  expensive branches [src:arxiv_1409.4842 p.5].
- An efficiency budget built into the design: most models were kept to about
  1.5 billion multiply-adds at inference so they could be used in practice,
  including on mobile and embedded devices [src:arxiv_1409.4842 p.1].
- 12× fewer parameters than the 2012 winner of Krizhevsky et al., while being
  much more accurate [src:arxiv_1409.4842 p.1].

## Method

- **Motivation.** Making a network uniformly bigger raises the risk of
  overfitting and increases compute quadratically. Sparse connectivity would
  solve this in principle, but current hardware is slow at sparse
  computation [src:arxiv_1409.4842 p.3]. Inception approximates a sparse
  structure with dense building blocks [src:arxiv_1409.4842 p.4].
- **Dimension reduction.** 1×1 convolutions, taken from Network-in-Network,
  mainly serve to remove computational bottlenecks, so the network can grow
  wider as well as deeper [src:arxiv_1409.4842 p.2]. Each one is also followed
  by a ReLU [src:arxiv_1409.4842 p.5].
- **GoogLeNet layout.** Ordinary convolutions in the lower layers, then nine
  Inception modules with max pooling between stages, then global average
  pooling, 40% dropout and a linear softmax classifier
  [src:arxiv_1409.4842 p.5] [src:arxiv_1409.4842 p.6].
- **Depth.** 22 layers with parameters, or 27 counting pooling. Replacing the
  fully connected layers with average pooling improved top-1 accuracy by about
  0.6% [src:arxiv_1409.4842 p.6].
- **Auxiliary classifiers.** Two side classifiers attached after modules 4a
  and 4d strengthen the gradient signal and regularize training. Their losses
  are weighted by 0.3 and they are removed at inference
  [src:arxiv_1409.4842 p.6].
- **Training.** CPU-based DistBelief with asynchronous SGD and momentum 0.9;
  the learning rate drops 4% every 8 epochs, and the final model uses Polyak
  averaging. The authors estimate a few high-end GPUs could train it in a week
  [src:arxiv_1409.4842 p.8].
- **Augmentation.** Random crops covering 8–100% of the image area, with aspect
  ratio between 3/4 and 4/3, plus photometric distortions
  [src:arxiv_1409.4842 p.8].

## Results

- **Classification.** First place at ILSVRC 2014 with 6.67% top-5 error and no
  outside data. This is 56.5% lower than SuperVision in 2012 and about 40%
  lower than Clarifai in 2013 [src:arxiv_1409.4842 p.9].
- **Test-time cost.** That result came from 7 models × 144 crops, 1008 times the
  cost of one crop on one model. One model on one crop scores 10.07%, and one
  model on 144 crops scores 7.89% [src:arxiv_1409.4842 p.9].
- **Detection.** First place with 43.9% mAP using an ensemble of 6 networks
  [src:arxiv_1409.4842 p.10]. The pipeline is R-CNN with Inception as the
  region classifier, with region proposals from Selective Search combined with
  multi-box, and no bounding-box regression [src:arxiv_1409.4842 p.10].
- **Detection, single model.** Table 5 reports 38.02% mAP, but the text says
  the ensemble improves results "from 40% to 43.9%". The paper gives both
  numbers [src:arxiv_1409.4842 p.10].

## Limitations

- **Unproven design principle.** The authors say it is still unclear whether
  the gains really come from the sparse-structure principles behind the design
  [src:arxiv_1409.4842 p.4].
- **Unclear training recipe.** Image sampling changed over the months before
  the competition, so the authors cannot say which single training recipe is
  best [src:arxiv_1409.4842 p.8].
- **Shared initial weights.** The 7 ensemble models started from the same
  initial weights, which the authors call an oversight
  [src:arxiv_1409.4842 p.8].
- **Manual tuning.** Trading accuracy for speed currently needs careful manual
  design [src:arxiv_1409.4842 p.5].
